# Blackcrab 0.3.0: a calmer desktop, and your sessions in your pocket

Blackcrab 0.3.0 is the biggest release yet. The desktop app gets a round of
features that make long days of Claude Code work easier to navigate, and it
lays the groundwork for something people have asked for repeatedly: getting to
your sessions when you're away from your machine.

## A more capable desktop

This release folds in a batch of desktop improvements:

- **Read-only session view with live tailing** — follow a running session
  without taking it over, watching new output stream in.
- **Diagnostics console** — recent errors are recorded in one place, so when
  something misbehaves you have a trail instead of a guess.
- **A bigger command palette** — keyword search across your sessions plus more
  actions, so the keyboard can do more of the work.
- **Saved grid layouts** — arrange the conversations you watch together and
  bring that layout back with a palette command.
- **Backup & restore** — export and re-import your settings, layouts, usage
  history, drafts, and per-session overrides. Transcripts and secrets stay out
  of the backup.
- **Session-conflict handling** — clearer behavior when a conversation is
  already open somewhere else, instead of two writers fighting over one file.

There are also quieter fixes that matter in daily use: resuming an older
conversation no longer fails when its original model has been retired, and
scrolling through a long transcript is smooth instead of jumpy.

## Coming soon: Blackcrab Remote for iPhone and Android

The headline of 0.3.0 isn't on the desktop at all. **Blackcrab Remote**, a
companion mobile app, is coming soon to **iPhone and Android** — and the host
side that makes it work ships in this desktop release.

The idea is simple: your Claude Code sessions live on your computer, but you
shouldn't have to be sitting at it to keep an eye on them or nudge them along.
Blackcrab Remote connects your phone to your desktop so you can do exactly that.

Here's what it does:

- **Pair in seconds.** Scan a QR code from the desktop (or paste a short
  payload) and your phone is linked. Connections are end-to-end encrypted, work
  directly over your local Wi-Fi, and fall back to an encrypted relay when
  you're away from home.
- **See every session.** Your conversations show up as a chat list, with unread
  indicators and the most recent activity surfaced first.
- **Read transcripts beautifully.** Messages render as real formatted text —
  headings, bold, lists, and code blocks — and the model's tool calls (running
  a command, editing a file, searching the codebase) appear as clean,
  terminal-style cards instead of walls of raw text.
- **Reply and steer from anywhere.** Send a follow-up message, stop a runaway
  turn, or approve and deny permission prompts right from your pocket. Your
  messages appear instantly, with a "thinking" indicator while the model works.
- **Start and continue work remotely.** Kick off a brand-new session in any of
  your projects from the phone, or jump back into a past conversation — the
  desktop resumes it for you.
- **Pick up where you left off, on either device.** A synced read cursor means
  the conversation opens to the first thing you haven't seen yet, whether you
  last read it on your desktop or your phone. A "— new messages —" divider shows
  you exactly where you stopped.

A conversation that's actively running in another window is flagged and left
alone, so the phone never collides with a live session.

Blackcrab Remote is heading to TestFlight first, with the App Store and Google
Play to follow. We'll share links as soon as they're live.

## Upgrade notes

There are no manual migration steps for 0.3.0. Existing users can update through
the app or download a fresh installer from GitHub Releases. As with earlier
builds, the release is macOS-first, with Windows and Linux artifacts produced by
CI for testing.
