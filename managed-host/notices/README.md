The objc2 family publishes crates without its workspace LICENSE.md. Registry
entries pin each crate version to its published .cargo_vcs_info.json revision
and the exact upstream workspace notice. The package includes that notice,
original Cargo metadata/authors and the standard MIT license text (selected from
the declared alternatives). The SPDX template remains unchanged, including its
placeholders; they do not assert an invented copyright holder.
